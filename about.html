India's Next-Gen Geospatial Technology Company

Vaayu Geo Technologies Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated in May 2026 with a singular mission: to deliver precision geospatial intelligence that transforms how India's mining, infrastructure, and government sectors make decisions.

Our team brings over 20 years of combined, hands-on experience in drone operations, LiDAR processing, photogrammetry, and GIS — with a deep specialisation in mining surveys across Odisha, Jharkhand, and Chhattisgarh, and international project exposure spanning Saudi Arabia, Germany, Spain, the UK, and Canada.

We don't just deliver data — we build AI-powered intelligence platforms, develop custom GIS solutions, and actively research autonomous robotics for the mining sector.

// RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Pioneering AI & Robotics for the Mining Sector

At Vaayu Geo Technologies, we are not just service providers — we are actively building the next generation of mining intelligence technology. Our R&D programme focuses on three converging frontiers: AI-driven geospatial analytics, digital twin infrastructure, and autonomous drone robotics for mine environments.

We are developing a proprietary AI-powered mining intelligence platform, encompassing volumetric analysis, haul road intelligence, compliance monitoring, and digital twin visualisation for open-cast mine sites.

🧠 Mining AI

Automated terrain classification, anomaly detection, predictive analytics for mine fleet and haul road optimisation using drone-derived data.

🔁 Digital Twin

Real-time 3D digital twin environments for open-cast mines — integrating LiDAR, photogrammetry, and sensor data streams via CesiumJS.

🤖 Autonomous Robotics

Research into autonomous UAV missions, obstacle avoidance, and underground mine inspection using LiDAR-equipped robotic platforms.
