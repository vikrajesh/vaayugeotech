// WHAT WE DO

Our Services

End-to-end geospatial and drone solutions — from raw data acquisition to AI-driven analytics and GIS platform development.

🛸
Drone Survey & UAV Operations

End-to-end UAV-based mapping and data acquisition with DGCA-certified pilots. Flight planning, multi-sensor payloads (RGB, multispectral, LiDAR), and precision delivery for mining, infrastructure, and government programmes.

RGB Mapping Multispectral LiDAR UAV DGCA Certified Fleet Ops
🏔️
LiDAR Data Processing

Full-pipeline airborne and ground-based LiDAR processing — point cloud classification, DTM/DSM generation, contour production, and volumetric analysis using TBC, TerraModeler, and custom Python/PDAL workflows.

Point Cloud DTM/DSM TerraModeler PDAL 3D Terrain
📐
Photogrammetry

Dense photogrammetric reconstruction for topographic mapping, cadastral surveys, and volumetric applications. High-accuracy orthomosaics and 3D models delivered using Agisoft Metashape, Pix4D, and LPS.

Metashape Pix4D Orthomosaic LPS 3D Models
⛏️
Mining Drone Surveys

Specialised drone survey solutions for open-cast and underground mines — stockpile volumetrics, pit volume estimation, overburden tracking, haul road gradient analysis, compliance mapping, and Coal India-format deliverables.

Volumetrics Haul Road Stockpile Coal India Iron Ore
🌐
GIS Development & Spatial Analytics

Custom GIS application development, spatial database design, and geospatial analytics platforms. PostGIS-backed web GIS, ArcGIS/QGIS automation, Python-GDAL pipelines, and CesiumJS 3D geospatial viewers.

ArcGIS PostGIS CesiumJS Python/GDAL Web GIS
🤖
Mining AI & Robotics

AI-powered mining intelligence — automated terrain classification, anomaly detection, predictive haul-road analysis, digital twin development, and autonomous drone robotics integration for next-generation mine operations.

AI Terrain Digital Twin Autonomous UAV Anomaly Detection Mine AI
